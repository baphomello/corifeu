# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this community open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and
healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from
  them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## A note on technical disagreement

Disagreeing with a decision is not a conduct problem. This project runs on recorded
decisions and stated reasoning — see [docs/adr/](docs/adr/) — and arguing against one with
evidence is exactly how it is meant to work.

What matters is that the disagreement is about the decision. Attack the argument as hard as
you like; leave the person alone.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and will
take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they find inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will explain their
reasoning when doing so.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, and also when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer at
**baphomello@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly.

Maintainers must respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in deciding consequences:

1. **Correction.** A private, written warning explaining the violation and why the behaviour
   was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including no
   interaction with those involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from public interaction within the community.
